# 非圆周区域
已知非圆周区域D，求$\displaystyle\iint\limits_{D}f(x,y)dxdy$
1、分类区域D：做一条垂直于x轴的直线，与D的**边界最多只有两个交点**，则为$D_{x}$
同理，做一条垂直于y轴的直线，与D的**边界最多只有两个交点**，则为$D_{y}$
![[Screenshot_20260304_111247.jpg]]

$\displaystyle\iint\limits_{Dx}f(x,y)dxdy=\int_{a}^{b}  \, dx\int_{y_{1}(x)}^{y_{2}(x)}f(x,y)  \, dy$
$\displaystyle\iint\limits_{Dy}f(x,y)dxdy=\int_{c}^{d}  \, dy\int_{x_{1}(y)}^{x_{2}(y)}f(x,y)  \, dx$
先将后面的积出来，再代入前面的积分计算

# 题目

$求\displaystyle \iint\limits_{D}xy\,dxdy\qquad D由x=2,y=1,y=x围成$
$$
\begin{align*}
画出草图，判断D的类型，发现都可以，按D_{x}做\quad\qquad\qquad\\ \\
a=1,b=2,y_{1}(x)=1,y_{2}(x)=x\quad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\\ \\
\iint\limits_{D}xy\,dxdy=\int_{1}^{2}  \, dx\int_{1}^{x}  xy\, dy\quad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad\qquad\\ \\
先算后面: \int_{1}^{x}  xy\, dy=x\int_{1}^{x} y \, dy=x\left[  \frac{1}{2} y^{2} \right]_{1}^x=x\left(  \frac{1}{2}x^{2}-\frac{1}{2}  \right)\qquad\\ \\
代入前面: \int_{1}^2{}  x\left(  \frac{1}{2}x^{2}-\frac{1}{2}  \right)\, dx =\frac{1}{2}\int_{1}^2 ( x^{3}-x ) \, dx =\frac{1}{2}\left[ \frac{1}{4}x^4-\frac{1}{2}x^{2} \right]_{1}^2\\ \\
结果为 \frac{9}{8}\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad
\end{align*}
$$

如果按$D_{y}$做
$$
\begin{align*}
c=1,d=2,x_{1}(y)=y,x_{2}(y)=2\qquad\qquad\\ \\
\iint\limits_{D}xy\,dxdy=\int_{1}^{2}  \, dy\int_{y}^{2}  xy\, dx\qquad\qquad\\ \\
\int_{y}^{2}  xy\, dx=y\int_{y}^2 x  \, dx =y\left[  \frac{1}{2} x^{2} \right]_{y}^2=y\left( 2- \frac{1}{2}y^{2} \right)\\ \\
\int_{1}^{2} y\left( 2- \frac{1}{2}y^{2} \right) \, dy=\left[ y^{2}-\frac{1}{8} y^4\right]_{1}^2 \qquad\\ \\
结果为 \frac{9}{8}\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad
\end{align*}
$$
# 更高难度的题目

某些积分如$\displaystyle\int \frac{\sin x}{x} \, dx ,\, \int \frac{\cos x}{x}dx,\, \int e^{ -x^{2} }dx,\,\int e^{ x^{2} }dx$
无法算出，遇到时需要改变D的类型

$\displaystyle求\iint\limits_{D} \frac{\sin y}{y}dxdy,D由x=y^{2},y=x围成$
$若按照D_{x}来做$
$$
\begin{align*}
a=0,b=1,y_{1}(x)=x,y_{2}(x)=\sqrt{ x }\\ \\
\iint\limits_{D} \frac{\sin y}{y}dxdy=\int_{0}^{1}  \, dx\int_{x}^{\sqrt{ x }} \frac{\sin y}{y}dy\\ \\
后面的无法计算，不能按照D_{x}来做
\end{align*}
$$
尝试$D_{y}$
$$
\begin{align*}
c=0,d=1,x_{1}(y)=y^{2},x_{2}(y)=y\qquad\qquad\quad\\ \\
\iint\limits_{D} \frac{\sin y}{y}dxdy=\int_{0}^{1}  \, dy\int_{y^{2}}^{y} \frac{\sin y}{y}dx\qquad\quad\qquad\\ \\
\int_{y^{2}}^{y} \frac{\sin y}{y}dx= \frac{\sin y}{y}\int_{y^{2}}^{y} 1 \, dx= \frac{\sin y}{y}[x]_{y^{2}}^y=\frac{\sin y}{y}(y-y^{2})\\ \\
\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\sin y}{y}(y-y^{2}) \, dy =\int_{0}^{1}\sin y(1-y)  \, dy\qquad\qquad\\ \\
结果为1-\sin 1\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad
\end{align*}
$$

# 圆周区域
当D与圆有关，或被积函数含有$x^{2}+y^{2}$时，利用极坐标计算
$\displaystyle\iint\limits_{D}f(x,y)dxdy,令x=r\cos\theta,y=r\sin\theta$，于是原式变为$\displaystyle\iint\limits_{D}f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)\,r\quad dr d\theta$
把$r,\theta$的范围解出来，接下来就是[[非圆周区域上二重积分的计算#^b363f0|计算]],**先对$r$积分**
注意$f(x,y)$换元后要乘$r$

# 题目

$求\displaystyle\iint\limits_{D}\sqrt{ x^{2}+y^{2} }dxdy,D: a^{2}\leq x^{2}+y^{2}\leq b^{2}$

$$
\begin{align*}
令x=r\cos\theta,y=r\sin\theta \quad\qquad\quad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\\ \\
原式=\iint\limits_{D}r^{2}dr d\theta \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad\qquad\qquad\\ \\
a\leq r\leq b,0\leq\theta\leq 2\pi \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\\ \\
\iint\limits_{D}r^{2}dr d\theta=\int_{0}^{2 \pi}  \, d\theta \int_{a}^{b}  \,r^{2} dr\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\\ \\  
\int_{a}^{b}  \,r^{2} dr=\left[ \frac{1}{3}r^3 \right]_{a}^b=\left( \frac{1}{3}b^3-\frac{1}{3}a^3 \right)\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad\\ \\
\int_{0}^{2 \pi} \left( \frac{1}{3}b^3-\frac{1}{3}a^3 \right) \, d\theta=\left( \frac{1}{3}b^3-\frac{1}{3}a^3 \right)\int_{0}^{2 \pi} 1 \, d\theta =\left( \frac{1}{3}b^3-\frac{1}{3}a^3 \right)2 \pi 
\end{align*}
$$


$r与\theta$的范围画图有助于理解

# 题目二

$求\displaystyle \iint\limits_{D}xydxdy,D:1\leq x^{2}+y^{2}\leq  2x,y\geq 0$
先算$r与\theta$的范围
$$
\begin{align*}
令x=r\cos\theta,y=r\sin\theta \qquad\qquad\\ \\
1\leq r^{2}\leq 2r\cos\theta,\quad r\sin\theta\geq 0\qquad\\ \\
由1\leq r^{2}\leq 2r\cos\theta得1\leq r\leq 2\cos\theta \quad\\ \\
因为r\geq  1,所以\cos\theta \geq \frac{r}{2}\geq \frac{1}{2}\qquad\\ \\
又因为r\sin\theta\geq 0，所以0\leq\theta\leq \frac{\pi}{3}\quad\\
\end{align*}
$$
接下来直接计算
$$
\begin{align*}
原式=\iint\limits_{D}r^{3}\cos\theta \sin\theta \quad dr d\theta
=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\cos\theta \sin\theta  \, d\theta \int_{1}^{2\cos\theta} r^{3}\, dr\qquad\\ \\
 \int_{1}^{2\cos\theta} r^{3} \, dr=  = \left[ \frac{1}{4}r^4 \right]_{1}^{2\cos\theta}=\ \left( 4\cos^ 4\theta- \frac{1}{4} \right) \qquad\qquad\\ \\
 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}  \cos\theta \sin\theta  \left( 4\cos^ 4\theta- \frac{1}{4} \right)\, d\theta =4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}  \cos^5\theta\ \sin\theta \,d\theta- \frac{1}{4}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}  \cos\theta \sin\theta \,d\theta\\ \\=-4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}  \cos^5\theta\  \,d\cos\theta+ \frac{1}{4}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}  \cos\theta  \,d\cos\theta\\ \\
 =-\frac{2}{3}[\cos^6\theta]_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}+ \frac{1}8{}[\cos^2\theta]_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}= \frac{9}{16}\qquad\qquad
\end{align*}
$$
